# Adaptive Signals and Visit Preparation

## Five adaptive signal functions

Search terms, source priority, weighting, and sales implication must be generated from industry, sub-sector, business model, lifecycle, product layer, buyer role, known vendors, and market context.

| Function | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Growth/revenue | What does the customer need to grow, sell, expand, or invest in? |
| Core business change | What changed in products, customers, channels, delivery, or operations? |
| Technology/data need | Where are AI, cloud, data, development, compute, or systems changing? |
| Procurement/ecosystem | What changed in tenders, vendors, partnerships, hiring, M&A, or delivery? |
| Risk/operating constraints | What changed in regulation, cost, data, security, organization, finance, or budget? |

Examples: finance prioritizes products/pricing, channels, risk, fintech procurement, regulation/data; manufacturing prioritizes orders, plants/supply chain, quality/R&D, MES/ERP/industrial AI, cost/safety; energy prioritizes assets, grid/trading, operations, dispatch/compute, policy; internet prioritizes product, users/revenue, engineering/model capability, cloud/partners, cost/compliance; retail prioritizes product/marketing, stores, membership/supply chain, CRM/service/data, operating/consumer protection; healthcare prioritizes pipeline, trial/registration, research data/AI, privacy; logistics prioritizes customers/capacity, route/network, dispatch, IoT/cloud/algorithm, safety/cost.

## News ranking

    industry relevance × customer-business relevance × proposed-product relevance ×
    buyer-role relevance × recency × event magnitude × source credibility

Priority 1: directly relevant customer/peer project, budget, procurement, cooperation, or release.
Priority 2: growth, revenue, customer, channel, delivery, or operations pressure.
Priority 3: regulatory, compliance, cost, organization, finance, or technical feasibility changes.
Priority 4: supplier, competitor, group, or ecosystem changes.
Priority 5: general news only if stronger evidence is absent.

For every item show fact, direct source/date, why customer cares, product implication, a natural sentence, and a follow-up question. If no strong current item exists, say so and use 30/90-day context rather than inventing a topic.

Track reporting/strategy, budget/tender/renewal/acceptance, product/channel/campaign/expansion, system go-live/migration/hiring/vendor awards, and regulation/audit/data/security/model governance.

## Visit briefing and talk tracks

Default briefing: 7-day strong updates (max 3), 30-day important updates (max 5), 90-day background updates (max 5). Each contains fact, source/date, customer relevance, product implication, talk track, and open question.

First contact pattern:

    可核验的公开变化
    → 对客户业务的克制理解
    → 一个小、具体、可回答的问题

Business roles: growth, policy, channel, experience, operations.
IT/engineering: iteration, testing, interfaces, reliability, repeated work.
Security/compliance: data boundary, private access, permissions, audit, human review.
Vendor/SI: joint delivery and work reduction, never seizing their delivery role.

Never claim internal knowledge, group relationships as customer purchases, guaranteed compliance, or mandatory core-system replacement. First contact seeks one answer or a short meeting, not a large project.
