# B2B Sales Intelligence

Use public information to make a product-to-customer sales decision, not a generic account profile.

## Inputs

Require 客户 and 我方产品/方案. Accept optional seller, product description/link, goal, visit role/date, geography, existing relationship, known vendors, and constraints. If public sources cannot establish the product's capabilities, state assumptions and request a product description or link.

## Required result

Decide sell / sell conditionally / nurture / partner / avoid. Identify concrete workflow, exclusions, suppliers and partners, same-layer competitors, evidence and uncertainty, timing, buyer roles, pilot, current news, and talk tracks.

## Evidence and safety

- Use only publicly accessible information. Never bypass logins, paywalls, CAPTCHAs, robots, or access controls; never use leaked, private, or personal data.
- Prefer the last 12 months for business/news/hiring/procurement and the last three years for vendors/cooperation. Mark older evidence as historical and current status unverified.
- Every material fact needs source, date, direct link, and grade: A customer/regulator/exchange/government; B vendor official or joint announcement; C credible media/association; D lead only.
- Explicitly label 公开事实, 判断, 建议目标, 客户待确认. Do not treat a group relationship as a relationship with the named legal entity.

## Workflow

1. Read [decision framework](references/decision-framework.md) before fit, supplier, case, competitor, or score analysis.
2. Read [adaptive signals and visits](references/signals-and-visit.md) for monitoring, timing, current news, or talk tracks.
3. Apply the relationship gate first. Direct competitors and coopetition accounts require a strategic gate, not a normal score.
4. Build each scenario as public fact → active workflow → pressure/change → product entry point → validation question.
5. Separate confirmed customer suppliers, group-only relationships, market competitors, and unverified leads.
6. Compare only relevant same-layer alternatives and say whether to compete, enter incrementally, partner, or avoid.
7. Lead with the decision, confidence, and smallest next action. Never invent a customer budget, deployment, contract, or use case.
